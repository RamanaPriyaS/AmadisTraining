How to Play Guess Game

Welcome to Guess Game !!

Rules

  1. The computer will randomly choose a secret number between 1 and 10.
  2. Your goal is to guess the correct number.
  3. Enter one guess at a time.
  4. If your guess is incorrect, you'll be asked to try again.
  5. Every guess counts as one attempt.
  6. Once you win, the game displays:
    • Your Name
    • Secret Number
    • Total Number of Attempts
